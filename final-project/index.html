All about Kendra Scott

Kendra Scott is a jewelry designer and businesswoman. She founded her own jewlery brand with just five hundred dollars. Luckily her brand took off and became worth millions.

Popular Items

Kendra Scott is known for her jewelry. Her most popular items are her earrings. Her earrings come in many different styles and colors. They are also very affordable. Her most popular earrings are the Elle earrings, the Danielle earrings, and the Rayne earrings. The Elle earrings are a simple teardrop shape that come in many different colors. The Danielle earrings are a more bold look with a geometric shape. The Rayne earrings are a more classic look with a round shape.
